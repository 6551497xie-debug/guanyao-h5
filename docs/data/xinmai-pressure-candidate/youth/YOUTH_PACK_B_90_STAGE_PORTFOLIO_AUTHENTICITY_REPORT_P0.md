# YOUTH Content Pack B 90 — Stage Portfolio Authenticity Report P0

YOUTH authenticity is encoded at portfolio level, not by making every item exclusive to ages 18–24. The pack uses observable access, term-setting, resource, relationship, household, group, and direction events without printing an age or maturity judgment.

## How the portfolio avoids YOUTH stereotypes

- Institutional entry and opaque rules appear alongside real responsibility; the user is never described as inexperienced by nature.
- Employment is one structure among platform work, short cooperation, independent payments, shared services, family coordination, relationships, community participation, digital authorship, and direction changes.
- Shared living and family arrangements are conditional scenes. The pack never assumes parents, marriage, children, or a particular residence.
- Resource constraints are contractual and observable—access, deposits, settlement, account permissions, schedules—not a claim that young adults are universally poor.
- Direction changes are expressed through current commitments and exit rights, not “finding oneself”, graduation, or first-time labels.
- The same six fields carry full stakes: authority, compensation, consent, household decisions, group reciprocity, body/time access, and work-role content. They are not lighter copies of MID_LIFE events.

Independent Product Control must still judge whether the 90-item combination feels broad, credible, and playable. This authoring report does not claim `PACK_ACCEPTED`.
